# externalCodeAudits/ — RQ1 trigger-pool baseline reading-pack

Single source of truth for the 6 audited methods so we don't re-read
the papers. Each entry summarizes **datasets used**, **novel
contribution**, and **implementation details that affect faithful
reproduction on Phi-4-mini / Gemma-4-26B-A4B-it / Qwen3.6-35B-A3B**.

The doc closes with two synthesis sections:

- **Burden of proof for our paper** — what these papers establish as
  the floor we must clear.
- **Ideas for future work** — gaps surfaced by reading the audit pool
  side-by-side.

PDFs are committed alongside each submodule directory:
`DRAGIN.pdf`, `FLARE.pdf`, `seakr.pdf`, `MBA.pdf`, `self-rag.pdf`,
`selfcheckgpt.pdf`.

## Per-method one-pagers

### 1. DRAGIN — Su et al. 2024 (ACL)

**Datasets.** 2WikiMHQA (multi-hop, distractor variant), HotpotQA
(multi-hop, distractor), IIRC (incomplete-information reading
comprehension), StrategyQA (commonsense).
**Why these.** All four require multi-step external retrieval; chosen
to expose where static retrieval (single-shot) is insufficient.
**How.** Standard EM/F1 on closed-book and dynamic-RAG modes with the
DRAGIN trigger; comparison against FLARE, SR-RAG (single-round),
FL-RAG (fixed-length), FS-RAG (fixed-sentence), IRCoT.

**Novel point.** Two contributions:
1. **Real-time information needs detection** (`RIND`): a per-token
   importance score `H_i × a_max(i) × s_i` — entropy weighted by how
   much later tokens attend to position `i`, masked to content tokens.
   Triggers when **any** token's RIND exceeds threshold τ.
2. **Query reformulation** uses *real-words attention* — the trigger
   token's attention back to prior content words selects the search
   query, not just the last sentence.

**Implementation details that matter for us.**
- Reads attention from **the last full-attention layer**.
  Documented extension on hybrid stacks: read the last *full-attention*
  layer, not the literal last layer (matters for Qwen-3.6 GDN-hybrid).
- `a_max(i)` = max over heads (the `oneal2000/DRAGIN` reference impl
  matches; paper text is ambiguous).
- Content filter `s_i` uses spaCy stopwords; subword fragments leak as
  content (known limitation, upstream same).
- Strict reproducibility checks in their README: incomplete
  Elasticsearch index causes silent 2–4 EM-point drift.
- Multi-GPU is required for paper-reported numbers (single-GPU
  reproduction breaks something they did not specify).

### 2. FLARE — Jiang et al. 2023 (EMNLP)

**Datasets.** Four long-form, knowledge-intensive generation tasks:
2WikiMHQA (multi-hop QA), StrategyQA (chain-of-thought commonsense),
ASQA (long-form open-domain QA with multiple correct answers), WikiAsp
(multi-aspect open-domain summarization).
**Why these.** Single-time retrieval suffices for short-form factoid
QA, but **long-form generation has information needs that are not
evident from the input alone**. The four tasks span different long-form
shapes (multi-hop reasoning, CoT, long-answer QA, multi-aspect summary)
to argue the trigger is task-shape-agnostic.
**How.** Two variants:
- **FLARE_instruct** (Toolformer-style): few-shot prompt the LM to
  emit `[Search(query)]` tokens; halt and retrieve when emitted.
- **FLARE_direct** (the headline): generate a temporary next sentence
  `s_t = LM(x, y_<t)` *without* retrieval; if **any** token in `s_t`
  has probability < θ, formulate a query from `s_t` (either by masking
  low-confidence tokens or by prompting GPT-3.5-turbo to generate a
  question targeting the low-confidence span) and re-decode the
  sentence with retrieved documents prepended.
Compared against single-shot RAG, fixed-length RAG (FL-RAG every n
tokens), fixed-sentence RAG (FS-RAG every sentence), and IRCoT.

**Novel point.** **Forward-looking active retrieval** — the trigger
fires on confidence in the *upcoming* sentence (a look-ahead drafted
by the model itself), not on confidence in already-generated tokens.
Sentences are chosen as the iteration unit because they are
"semantic units that are neither too short nor too lengthy."

**Implementation details that matter for us.**
- Trigger threshold `θ ∈ [0, 1]` controls firing rate (θ = 0 never
  retrieves; θ = 1 retrieves every sentence). Masking threshold β
  separately controls query-formulation aggressiveness.
- Trigger signal is per-token min-probability inside the look-ahead
  sentence — needs `max_logprob` per generated token in the dump
  (we currently have per-step entropy aggregates only).
- Backbone is GPT-3.5 (text-davinci-003) via API — black-box logprob
  access. Cross-model reproduction on Phi/Gemma/Qwen is straightforward
  since we control the decoder; per-token max-logprob is well-defined.
- Retriever: BM25 over the DPR Wikipedia dump for Wiki-grounded tasks;
  Bing Search API for open-web tasks. We will use BM25 (matches our
  existing pipeline).
- Two operating modes for the *re-decoding* behavior (truncate-at-prob
  vs replace-low-confidence-terms) are system-side, not signal-side;
  our audit uses only the trigger signal, not the re-decode policy.

### 3. SeaKR — Yao et al. 2024 (ACL)

**Datasets.** Complex: 2WikiMHQA, HotpotQA, IIRC. Simple: NQ,
TriviaQA, SQuAD. Open-domain setting (provided documents discarded;
model uses retrieved Wikipedia only).
**Why these.** Spans complex (multi-hop) and simple (single-hop) so
the claim is "self-aware uncertainty works on both."
**How.** Sample K=20 generations per rationale step, take the
hidden state of the `<EOS>` token from each, compute the regularized
Gram determinant `det(G + αI)` where `G[i,j] = h_i^T h_j`. Trigger if
determinant > δ. Re-rank retrieved knowledge by which snippet *reduces*
this self-aware uncertainty most.

**Novel point.** First adaptive-RAG method to use **internal-state
consistency** as the trigger. Argues that decoding loses information
versus continuous internal states; uncertainty estimated *in the
hidden space* is more substantial than logprob-level signals. Three
self-aware functions: (1) when to retrieve, (2) re-rank knowledge,
(3) choose between answer-from-rationales vs answer-from-knowledge.

**Implementation details that matter for us.**
- Headline trigger requires **K=20 stochastic samples × per-sample
  hidden-state capture at `<EOS>`** — this is the load-bearing capture
  burden, ~20× decode + storage of 20 hidden vectors per step.
- Backbone is LLaMA-2-7B-Chat; ships a custom vLLM fork
  (`vllm_uncertainty/`) to expose hidden states. Porting to Phi /
  Gemma / Qwen requires reading the sampler scaffold and replicating
  the same hook into our existing K=3 sampler in
  `scripts/crv/sota_capture.py`.
- Secondary signals computable from greedy logprobs are exposed in
  `SEAKR/utils.py:UncertaintyScore`: `perplexity`, `ln_entropy`
  (mean negative log-likelihood across K samples), `energy_score`
  (logsumexp of greedy logprobs). These are the proxies we already
  computed in length_audit_v2 (one of which is just `ent.max` renamed).
- Default eigen threshold is `−6.0` for LLaMA-2-7B-Chat on multi-hop;
  arch-specific re-calibration likely required.

### 4. Self-RAG — Asai et al. 2024 (ICLR)

**Datasets.** PopQA (long-tail factual QA), TriviaQA, PubHealth (fact
verification), ARC-Challenge (reasoning MCQ), ASQA (long-form QA),
FactScore-Bio (biographical generation). Six tasks across short-form
QA, long-form generation, fact verification, reasoning.
**Why these.** Argues the trained reflection-token vocabulary
generalizes across task families; long-form FactScore-Bio
specifically shows citation-accuracy gains.
**How.** Train an LM to emit special tokens — `[Retrieve]`, `[No
Retrieve]`, relevance/support/utility ratings — interleaved with task
output. At inference, segment-wise beam search optimizes a weighted
combination of these reflection-token probabilities.

**Novel point.** **Trained reflection tokens** — the LM learns *as
part of language modeling* both when to retrieve and how to critique
its own output. Distinguishes "retrieve-on-demand" (per segment) from
question-level routing.

**Implementation details that matter for us.**
- Released checkpoints: `selfrag-LLaMA-2-7B`, `selfrag-LLaMA-2-13B`.
  No checkpoints exist for Phi / Gemma / Qwen; reflection-token
  vocabulary requires a multi-day fine-tune per architecture.
- Training pipeline depends on a separate **critic LLM** (uses
  GPT-4-style annotations distilled to reflection tokens).
- For our audit: the only viable integration is to run the released
  LLaMA-2-7B checkpoint on our HotpotQA-500 / 2Wiki-500 slices as a
  separate-model reference row. Inference is cheap (~2 h on one GPU).
- vLLM-only: needs `skip_special_tokens=False` to expose the reflection
  tokens.

### 5. SelfCheckGPT — Manakul et al. 2023 (EMNLP)

**Datasets.** WikiBio passages generated by GPT-3 (text-davinci-003),
**manually annotated** at sentence level for factual / partially-
factual / hallucinated. ~239 passages, ~1908 sentences. Released
publicly.
**Why this.** Designed to evaluate *zero-resource* hallucination
detection; WikiBio long-tail entities mean the LLM is genuinely
uncertain about many of them.
**How.** Generate K=20 stochastic completions of the same prompt at
high temperature. Score each sentence in the original output by its
agreement with the K samples using one of:
1. **BERTScore** of the sentence vs each sample;
2. **MQAG** — generate Q-A pairs from the sentence, ask each sample
   for the answer, count disagreements;
3. **n-gram** — average negative log-prob of the sentence under
   unigram / bigram models built from the K samples;
4. **NLI** (added 2023.11) — DeBERTa-v3-large-MNLI prob(contradiction)
   between the sentence and each sample;
5. **LLM-prompt** — GPT-3.5/4 yes/no factuality verifier.

**Novel point.** **Trace-level zero-resource** consistency checking —
no logprobs, no retrieval database, no fine-tuning. Works on black-box
APIs.

**Implementation details that matter for us.**
- Headline variant in their README is **NLI** (DeBERTa-v3-large-MNLI,
  released).
- For our audit: requires K=3 (or higher) sample completion **texts**
  per step. Currently we keep the aggregated `u_cons` only and discard
  the texts; one-knob extension to the K=3 sampler dumps them.
- Aggregation: paper's `score_per_sentence` is mean over samples; we
  will aggregate to trace-level by max over steps to match the audit
  protocol.
- MQAG variant is heavier (Q-A model + answer model); we audit only
  NLI primary + n-gram secondary.

### 6. MBA-RAG — Tang et al. 2024 (NAACL workshop)

**Datasets.** Six. Single-hop: SQuAD, NaturalQuestions, TriviaQA.
Multi-hop: MuSiQue, HotpotQA, 2WikiMHQA. Same six as Adaptive-RAG
(Jeong 2024) for direct comparability.
**Why these.** Single-hop expose unnecessary-retrieval costs;
multi-hop expose insufficient-retrieval failures. Together they
let the bandit's reward function trade off accuracy vs steps.
**How.** Generator is **FLAN-T5-XL (3B)** with three retrieval arms:
no-RAG, single-shot RAG, IRCoT-style iterative RAG (3 rounds).
Bandit is **DistilBERT (66M)** or **T5-Large**, trained with
epsilon-greedy + dynamic reward `accuracy − cost-of-steps`.
Retriever is BM25.

**Novel point.** Replaces Adaptive-RAG's single-class classifier
(supervised on "the cheapest correct strategy") with a **multi-armed
bandit** over the 3 arms, allowing multiple correct strategies and
explicitly costing retrieval steps in the reward.

**Implementation details that matter for us.**
- Per-question signal (not per-step) — different granularity than
  every other method in the audit.
- Requires **per-arch retraining** of the BERT classifier because the
  reward depends on the underlying generator's accuracy; we'd train
  one classifier per (arch ∈ {Phi, Gemma, Qwen} × dataset).
- Training is fast: ~2 h GPU per (arch × dataset) cell.
- Inherits Adaptive-RAG's data-prep pipeline; same dev splits we use.

## Burden of proof for our paper (synthesized)

What the audited papers establish as the **floor** an EMNLP-short
trigger / evaluation paper has to clear:

### Datasets
- **At least 2 multi-hop QA benchmarks**, with HotpotQA-distractor +
  2WikiMHQA the de-facto pair (DRAGIN, SeaKR, MBA-RAG, FLARE all use
  both). Adding **MuSiQue** is now standard for 2024+ papers (MBA,
  Adaptive-RAG). IIRC adds an "incomplete-info" stress test (DRAGIN,
  SeaKR).
- **At least 1 zero-shot OOD or held-out generalization signal**.
  We have 2WikiMHQA as OOD; MuSiQue would add a second.

### Backbones
- The minimum bar in 2024 was LLaMA-2-7B-Chat alone. The 2025+ bar is
  **2 backbones at minimum**, ideally one dense + one MoE. **Our
  3-arch pool (Phi dense, Gemma MoE-sliding, Qwen MoE-hybrid-SSM) is
  ahead of the literature norm** — unique selling point for the paper.

### Metrics
- EM, F1 are the canonical trigger-paper metrics (SeaKR, DRAGIN,
  MBA, Self-RAG all report).
- **AUROC for retrieval-helpfulness** is *not* standard in trigger
  papers — they report end-task EM. Our `lift_em=+1` AUROC is closer
  to SelfCheckGPT's evaluation (sentence-level factuality AUROC) and
  is a methodological contribution if framed correctly.
- **Cost / step-count** is increasingly reported (MBA-RAG explicitly,
  DRAGIN partially). Our `tokens_generated` / `wall_clock_s` rows
  speak to this.
- **Bonferroni-corrected paired bootstrap** is *not* standard in
  this literature. Adding it is a methodological strengthening (this
  is the residualization protocol's contribution).

### Trigger-pool comparisons
- Standard comparison set in 2024: **DRAGIN, FLARE, SeaKR,
  Self-RAG, IRCoT, no-RAG, single-shot-RAG, fixed-sentence-RAG**.
  Our pool covers DRAGIN, FLARE, SeaKR, Self-RAG (as ref),
  SelfCheckGPT (newer), MBA-RAG (newer); we do not need IRCoT
  (orthogonal — multi-step orchestration, not a trigger).
- **DioR, ATLAS, Layered-Query-Retrieval, Adaptive-RAG, CoRAG** are
  the methods we considered and dropped; cite all of them in §2 with
  one-sentence scope-out.

### Statistical hardening
- Most trigger papers report a single seed and no significance test.
  **Reporting paired bootstrap CIs (and Bonferroni correction across
  the trigger pool) is a strict improvement** and one of our
  contributions.

### Cost / fairness
- **Cost-of-trigger** is the missing axis in the literature. SeaKR
  buries the K=20 cost; DRAGIN buries the attention-capture cost;
  Self-RAG buries the fine-tuning cost. **Our paper should surface
  this as part of the residualization framing** — length-controlled
  evaluation also implicitly controls for compute cost (longer
  decodes cost more).

### Net burden assessment
We are **at or above** the field's burden on every axis except
*end-task EM gains from a deployed trigger* (which our paper is not
trying to claim — we are evaluating triggers, not deploying them).
The methodology contribution (residualization protocol + Bonferroni
trigger-pool re-ranking) is genuinely novel relative to the audit
pool: none of the 6 audited methods control for length or trigger
cost in their published comparisons.

## Ideas for future work (surfaced by the side-by-side reading)

1. **Hidden-state geometry beyond Gram-determinant.** SeaKR uses
   `det(G + αI)` over K=20 `<EOS>` representations. Other geometric
   summaries (top-k eigenvalue ratio, entropy of singular spectrum,
   spectral radius) are unexplored as triggers. Cheap to compute once
   the K=20 capture exists.
2. **Cross-layer attention fusion (multi-layer DRAGIN).** DRAGIN
   reads one layer's attention. Atkinson / ICR / Deep-Thinking-Ratio
   argue different uncertainty types live in different layers.
   Concretely audit-able once we capture multi-layer attention in
   one re-decode.
3. **Trigger ensembles with disagreement coefficient.** Several
   methods cover disjoint failure modes (DRAGIN strong on
   compositional; entropy-position strong on early-fixable;
   length captures rambling). An ensembled trigger that fires on
   *signal disagreement* (rather than majority vote) is a natural
   follow-up. Our `external_only_gate.py` audit already started this.
4. **Question-side complexity router fused with step-level trigger.**
   MBA-RAG operates at question-level only; DRAGIN/SeaKR at
   step-level. A two-stage trigger (route by question complexity,
   then trigger by step uncertainty) is unexplored and free against
   our existing trace pool.
5. **Reflection-token transfer.** Self-RAG's reflection tokens are
   fine-tuned into LLaMA-2; can we **distill** them into a small
   adapter that bolts onto Phi/Gemma/Qwen without full fine-tuning?
   Adapter-only fine-tune of 0.1% of parameters might suffice.
6. **Cost-aware AUROC.** Replace AUROC's uniform cost assumption with
   a fire-rate-weighted variant: AUROC(τ) where the trigger
   acceptance threshold τ is fixed at the budget that maximizes
   `E[utility] = E[retrieval_helpful] − λ · fire_rate`. Lambda is
   the deployment cost knob.
7. **Benchmark-family stratification as standard practice.** Our
   `bridge_comparison` finding (length-correctness inversion on a
   subset) suggests every trigger paper should report
   per-question-type breakdowns — currently nobody does.
8. **Calibration gap.** All audited triggers use a single global
   threshold. Per-question-type calibration (4-dim hyperparameter
   on HotpotQA × 2Wiki types) is unexplored and can be tested on
   our existing traces with no new compute.
9. **Pure-SSM (Mamba) trigger.** None of DRAGIN/SeaKR work on
   non-attention architectures. Hidden-state-cosine-drift to
   question-anchor is a candidate fallback. Open research direction;
   would need a Mamba checkpoint added to the model pool.
10. **Trigger as feature for downstream re-ranking.** Combine our
    trigger signals with DynamicRAG's reranker — the trigger fires *and*
    informs the post-retrieval rerank. Two orthogonal contributions
    that would compose; pure paper-shape future-work bullet.
